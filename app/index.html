How every number is made

01 · Real prices

You pay what you'd really pay

Every bet buys at the real ask from Kalshi's minute-by-minute order book, $10 at a time, minus Kalshi's fee. Empty order books are never traded.

02 · No peeking

Only what you knew at the time

A rule sees prices up to the moment it fires and fills 1–2 minutes later. Automated tests rewrite the future of real games and check that no bet changes.

03 · Held-back test

Found on one period, judged on another

Ideas are searched on older games, chosen on newer ones, and tested once on the newest, with a correction for how many ideas were tried.
