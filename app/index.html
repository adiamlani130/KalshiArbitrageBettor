Where the data comes from. Prices are 1-minute bid/ask candles from Kalshi's public API. The first three datasets add real scores: ATP set results (Jan–Sep 2026) and ESPN NBA play-by-play (2025–26 season). All sports · price paths is prices only, for 14 leagues since August 2025: the price before the game and every 1–2 minutes during it. Game start times come from Kalshi's schedule; tennis and UFC starts are estimated from the burst in trading.

How bets are priced. You buy at the ask 1–5 minutes after the moment, $10 per bet, minus Kalshi's taker fee (7% × contracts × price × (1 − price), rounded up to the cent). "Before the match" tennis prices are the midpoint plus 1¢, because only midpoints were saved for that moment.

Research, not betting advice. Past prices don't guarantee future results, and small samples can look profitable by luck.
