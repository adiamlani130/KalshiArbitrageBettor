Markets vs Stocks

When a prediction market reprices a Fed cut, a tariff ruling or a shutdown, do the stocks that should care move first, at the same time, or late? Pick a Kalshi market and a stock, then test it six ways.

Pair

Lag-trade rule

Probabilities are read at 4:00pm New York time, the stock close, so "next day's open" is the first price you could really get.

Where the data comes from. Kalshi probabilities are hourly prices from Kalshi's public API (the bid/ask midpoint when the spread is 6¢ or less, otherwise the last trade). Only markets with at least 20,000 contracts traded and two weeks of life are included. Stock prices are Yahoo Finance daily opens and dividend-adjusted closes, plus hourly bars for the shock study.

How to read it. A link between a market and a stock is evidence, not proof. Hundreds of pairs are tested here, so some will look strong by luck. Trust a result more when it survives a cost, a hedge, both halves of the sample and other stocks in the same theme.
