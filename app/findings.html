What survived

Method. Each league's games are split by date: the first 60% to search, the last 40% held back. A fixed grid of rules is searched; a rule is kept only if it is clearly profitable in the search period (t-statistic 2+, 60+ bets). Kept rules are tested once on the held-back games and must pass a Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery rate of 10%. Market-stock pairs are found in the first half of their days and checked in the second half. Every bet is $10 at the ask, one sample (1–2 minutes) after the rule is seen, after Kalshi's fee.

Reproduce with python analysis/discover.py. Research, not betting or investment advice.
