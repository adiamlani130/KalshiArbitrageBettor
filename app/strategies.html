Strategies & Hedging

Six less obvious ways to use a prediction market: price it against the options market, trade the takeover spread against it, build baskets of second-order stocks, hedge a stock with a contract, trade the contract when the stock moves first, and bet the ladder before macro news.

Where the data comes from. Kalshi prices are from Kalshi's public API; stock, index, oil and crypto prices and the VIX, VXN and OVX volatility indexes are from Yahoo Finance. Bitcoin and ether use 30-day realized volatility because they have no free volatility index.

What "options-implied" means here. Real option chains could not be downloaded for this build, so the options market's view is rebuilt from its volatility index with a standard lognormal model and no skew. It prices far-away targets less reliably than a real call spread would. scripts/options_binary.py does the real call-spread calculation on live chains.

Research, not investment advice. Most tests here rest on a handful of independent outcomes, so read the pattern, not the decimals.
