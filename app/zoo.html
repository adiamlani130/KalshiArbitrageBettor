Method. Each strategy family has a small grid of settings. The best setting is chosen on the earlier period only (by t-statistic, with a minimum number of trades) and then graded once on the later period. Kalshi trades buy at the ask and sell at the bid of the next day's closing quote, after Kalshi's fee; held trades settle at $1 or $0. Stocks trade at the next day's open. "Days left" always uses the market's scheduled end, never the time it actually closed, because many markets close early when the event happens. "Works" means the result was positive in the earlier period and survived a 10% false-discovery correction (t ≥ 2) in the later one.
