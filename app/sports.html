Where the data comes from. Kalshi's public API: for each game, the bid and ask before the start (a day out, an hour out and just before) and every 1–2 minutes during it, for 14 leagues since August 2025. Start times come from Kalshi's schedule. Tennis matches and UFC fights rarely start on time, so their start is detected from the first burst of price moves, using only prices up to that minute.

How bets are priced. $10 per bet at the real ask, filled at the first live quote 1–2 minutes after the rule is seen (never at an empty order book), minus Kalshi's taker fee (7% × contracts × price × (1 − price), rounded up to the cent). Selling early sells at the bid and pays the fee again.

Prices only, no scores: a team's price falling is the market's read of the score. Research, not betting advice. Small samples can look profitable by luck; use the sensitivity check and the halves before trusting anything.
